1.
 
Synopsis
 
StockPro
 
is
 
a
 
full-stack
 
Inventory
 
Management
 
System
 
designed
 
for
 
mid-size
 
businesses
 
operating
 
across
 
multiple
 
warehouses
 
or
 
storage
 
locations.
 
The
 
platform
 
enables
 
businesses
 
to
 
gain
 
complete
 
visibility
 
into
 
their
 
stock
 
levels,
 
streamline
 
procurement
 
through
 
structured
 
purchase
 
order
 
workflows,
 
track
 
every
 
stock
 
movement
 
with
 
a
 
full
 
audit
 
trail,
 
and
 
proactively
 
manage
 
supply
 
gaps
 
through
 
intelligent
 
alerting
 
and
 
reorder
 
automation.
 
 
The
 
system
 
supports
 
four
 
primary
 
roles:
 
Warehouse
 
Staff
 
(who
 
manage
 
day-to-day
 
stock
 
operations),
 
Inventory
 
Managers
 
(who
 
oversee
 
products,
 
reports,
 
and
 
overall
 
inventory
 
health),
 
Purchase
 
Officers
 
(who
 
manage
 
supplier
 
relationships
 
and
 
procurement),
 
and
 
Administrators
 
(who
 
configure
 
the
 
system,
 
manage
 
users,
 
and
 
access
 
platform-wide
 
analytics).
 
Each
 
role
 
has
 
a
 
purpose-built
 
dashboard
 
with
 
the
 
controls
 
and
 
visibility
 
appropriate
 
to
 
their
 
function.
 
 
StockPro
 
is
 
architected
 
as
 
a
 
microservices
 
system
 
modelled
 
on
 
the
 
EShoppingZone
 
reference
 
pattern,
 
with
 
independent
 
services
 
for
 
authentication/user
 
management,
 
product/item
 
catalogue,
 
warehouse
 
and
 
stock
 
level
 
management,
 
purchase
 
order
 
lifecycle,
 
supplier
 
relationship
 
management,
 
stock
 
movement
 
audit
 
trail,
 
alert
 
and
 
notification
 
management,
 
and
 
inventory
 
analytics
 
and
 
reporting
 
—
 
all
 
integrated
 
through
 
a
 
Spring
 
MVC
 
website
 
controller
 
layer.

2.6
 
Stock
 
Movement
 
Requirements
 
•
 
Movement
 
types:
 
Stock
 
In
 
(GRN),
 
Stock
 
Out
 
(Issue),
 
Transfer
 
(between
 
warehouses),
 
Adjustment
 
(manual
 
correction),
 
Write-Off
 
(damaged/expired),
 
Return
 
(supplier
 
or
 
customer).
 
•
 
Every
 
movement
 
records:
 
product,
 
warehouse,
 
quantity,
 
unit
 
cost,
 
movement
 
type,
 
reference
 
(PO/issue
 
order),
 
performed
 
by,
 
and
 
timestamp.
 
•
 
Balance
 
after
 
movement
 
is
 
stored
 
to
 
enable
 
historical
 
stock-on-hand
 
reconstruction.
 
•
 
Movements
 
are
 
immutable
 
after
 
creation;
 
corrections
 
must
 
be
 
made
 
via
 
a
 
new
 
opposing
 
movement.
 
•
 
Movement
 
reports
 
can
 
be
 
exported
 
by
 
date
 
range,
 
product,
 
warehouse,
 
or
 
movement
 
type.
 
 
2.7
 
Alert
 
Requirements
 
•
 
Low-stock
 
alert
 
is
 
triggered
 
automatically
 
when
 
available
 
quantity
 
drops
 
below
 
the
 
product's
 
reorder
 
level.
 
•
 
Overstock
 
alert
 
is
 
triggered
 
when
 
quantity
 
exceeds
 
the
 
product's
 
maximum
 
stock
 
level.
 
•
 
PO
 
approval
 
pending
 
alert
 
is
 
sent
 
to
 
authorised
 
approvers
 
when
 
a
 
PO
 
is
 
submitted
 
for
 
approval.
 
•
 
Overdue
 
receipt
 
alert
 
is
 
sent
 
when
 
an
 
approved
 
PO's
 
expected
 
delivery
 
date
 
has
 
passed
 
without
 
a
 
GRN.
 
•
 
Alerts
 
carry
 
a
 
severity
 
level
 
(INFO,
 
WARNING,
 
CRITICAL)
 
and
 
can
 
be
 
acknowledged
 
by
 
the
 
recipient.
 
•
 
Email
 
notifications
 
are
 
sent
 
for
 
CRITICAL
 
alerts;
 
all
 
alerts
 
appear
 
in
 
the
 
in-app
 
alert
 
centre.

and

•
 
Daily

and
